How it finds the taps

No machine learning here, and you don't need any. A beat is an onset: a moment where energy in some frequency band jumps up fast. That's a signal-processing problem, and it's one you can solve exactly.

Every 10 ms the song is cut into a 1024-sample window, multiplied by a Hann window and run through an FFT. Take the magnitude spectrum, subtract the previous frame's, keep only the positive differences, and sum them inside a frequency band. That sum is the spectral flux of the band — it spikes when a drum lands in it.

Each band drives one finger, and each one gets its own adaptive threshold: a peak counts as a tap when it beats the local mean of the flux over a ±0.35 s window, multiplied by the sensitivity you set. Then a minimum gap per finger stops a single hit from registering twice.

The tempo readout comes from autocorrelating the summed flux: the lag between 60 and 200 BPM that correlates best with itself is the beat period.
